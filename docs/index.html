Dota Kill Clipper

A free, open-source OBS Studio script for Dota 2 highlight clips and YouTube Shorts.

Dota Kill Clipper runs on the player's own Windows PC inside OBS Studio. When the player gets a kill or an assist in their own Dota 2 match, it saves a short clip from the OBS replay buffer, renders a vertical 1080×1920 Short with background music, and can publish it to the player's own YouTube channel on a schedule.

Features

  • Kill and assist detection through Dota 2 Game State Integration.
  • Clips of kill streaks, sorted into per-match folders.
  • Vertical Shorts: centered gameplay, blurred background, call-to-action overlays, Kevin MacLeod music (CC BY 4.0, credited).
  • Upload to your own channel as private with a scheduled publish time, one slot every two hours from 08:00 to 22:00.
  • Playlists by kill count and a monthly review that changes weak videos to unlisted. Videos are never deleted.
YouTube API Services. Dota Kill Clipper uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and manage playlists on the channel you authorize.
