Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Dota Kill Clipper ("the script") is a free, open-source OBS Studio script that runs only on your own Windows computer. It records highlight clips of your own Dota 2 games and can publish them to your own YouTube channel. The developer operates no servers and does not receive any of your data.

1. YouTube API Services

The script uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel to the script you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Your data at Google is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information the script accesses

Only after you press "Log in to YouTube" and grant access, the script accesses:

  • the OAuth access token and refresh token issued by Google for your account;
  • the ID and title of the authorized channel, to confirm it is the channel configured in the script;
  • the list of videos uploaded to that channel with their privacy status, publish date and view count;
  • the titles and IDs of that channel's playlists.

The script does not access comments, subscribers, messages, payment data, or data of any other channel or user.

3. How the information is used

  • To upload clips you recorded, with a title, description, tags and a scheduled publish time.
  • To create playlists on your channel and add your videos to them.
  • Once a month, to compare the view counts of your own public videos older than 30 days and change clearly underperforming videos to "unlisted". The script never deletes videos.

The information is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose, and it is never sold.

4. Storage and retention

All data stays on your computer in %LOCALAPPDATA%\dota-kill-clipper:

  • youtube-token.json: your OAuth token;
  • jobs.json and schedule.json: the upload queue with the IDs of videos the script uploaded and the reserved publish times;
  • reports\review-YYYY-MM.json: monthly reports with video IDs and view counts. Reports older than 36 months are deleted automatically;
  • clipper.log: a local log file.

The script re-checks the authorization of your channel at least once a month. You can delete the folder at any time to remove all stored data.

5. Sharing

The script does not share your information with any third party. Data is sent only to Google and YouTube through the YouTube API, when the script performs the actions described above.

6. Cookies and device information

The script places no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. It only stores the local files listed in section 4 on your device.

7. Revoking access

You can revoke the script's access to your data at any time on the Google security settings page. You can also delete youtube-token.json from the folder above.

8. Contact

Questions or complaints: open an issue at github.com/Betreazen/dota-kill-clipper/issues.

9. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
